01 / About Sunea

Good Energy
Belongs Somewhere.

Somewhere, a home finds comfort in the warmth of light. Somewhere, a business finds the power to keep moving forward. And somewhere, a community takes a step toward a brighter future.

At SUNEA SOLAR & LIGHTING RETAIL OPC, we believe good energy belongs wherever life happens. It belongs in the spaces we call home, the work we pour our hearts into, and the places where new possibilities take shape.

Rooted in Pampanga, we bring this belief to life through quality lighting and solar energy solutions for homes, businesses, and industries. As we grow, our purpose remains the same: to make reliable energy more accessible through trusted products, thoughtful service, and genuine care.

Your Partner for a Brighter Tomorrow

We bring solar energy solutions closer to homes and businesses, helping customers explore more efficient ways to meet their energy needs. Alongside our lighting expertise, we remain committed to delivering quality products, practical options, and reliable service that customers can count on.

Our Vision

To become a trusted provider of solar and lighting solutions across the Philippines, recognized for quality, reliability, and customer service.

Our Mission

To deliver quality solar and lighting solutions that meet customer needs through dependable products, practical energy options, and responsive service, while building lasting relationships with homes, businesses, and industries nationwide.

03 / Built around the brief

Different roofs.
Different answers.

The most effective solar setup is never copy-pasted. We design around your site, energy goals, and power consumption—building an energy system engineered to last.
Take a look at how the SUNEA process works from site evaluation to after-sales care:

01 — DIAGNOSE

Assess the Site

We conduct an on-site ocular inspection and consultation to evaluate your roof structure, sun exposure, and energy consumption—setting a solid foundation for your setup.

02 — DESIGN

Shape the System

We design a tailored solar array and present a detailed quotation balanced against your daily energy load, budget, and target savings.

03 — DEPLOY

Make it last

From professional installation and commissioning to active performance monitoring and dedicated after-sales support, we ensure your investment delivers value long after launch.
