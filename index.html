01 / About Sunea

Replace banner.png with a Sunea company or project photo.

Good energy
belongs somewhere.

Somewhere, a home is waiting to feel brighter. Somewhere, a dream is waiting to grow. Somewhere, a future is waiting to begin. At SUNEA SOLAR & LIGHTING RETAIL OPC, we believe good energy belongs in all these places and in the lives of the people who bring them to life. Rooted in Pampanga, we provide lighting and solar energy solutions that help homes feel warmer, businesses move forward, and communities look toward a brighter future. As we continue to grow, our purpose remains the same: to bring reliable energy closer to the people who need it, with quality, care, and a genuine commitment to making a difference.
